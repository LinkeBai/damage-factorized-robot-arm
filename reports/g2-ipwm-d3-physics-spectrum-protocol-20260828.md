# IPWM D3 physics-spectrum audit protocol

## Purpose

Test whether the frozen seed27 IPWM result extends beyond the two aggregate
held-out mixtures to a transparent spectrum of residual physics while keeping
the joint-lock topology unseen. This is an audit expansion, not a development
set and not a replacement for the five-seed primary result.

## Frozen contract

- checkpoint: `runs/g2_r0_physical_context_residual/seed27_confirmation_v1/model.pt`;
- baseline: same-seed shared model plus analytic projection and matched rank-8 adapter;
- context: Z65 K25, posterior scale 1.38;
- rollout rule: eight-step grace and depth ramp 0.06;
- trajectories: 10 per D3 physics condition, 150 steps each;
- horizons: H10/H25/H50;
- targets/actions: paired and generated by the existing frozen goal protocol;
- no retraining, selection, threshold changes, or result-dependent exclusions.

## Conditions

`nominal`, `weak_motor`, `high_damping`, `delay_1`, `noisy_deadband`,
`mixed_composition`, and `mixed_unseen`, all under held-out D3.

## Metrics and interpretation

Primary: object RMSE relative to matched shared. Secondary: free-state RMSE,
pusher RMSE, overall RMSE, and exact lock violation. Raw rows are retained.

This audit strengthens the paper only if the direction is broadly stable. A
negative condition is retained and reported as a boundary. The two original
mixed domains and the five-seed audit remain the primary frozen statistical
claim; this seed27 spectrum is mechanism breadth, not a population estimate.

## Development outcome and frozen repair

Seed27 revealed that topology-only routing over-corrects D3 conditions whose
physical context remains close to the ordinary training support. Full IPWM was
negative at H25/H50 for nominal, weak-motor, delay-1, and noisy-deadband, while
the no-intervention path stayed near the matched baseline. Context norms also
separated the useful correction regimes in this development audit: nominal and
noisy-deadband were 0.507, weak-motor 0.769, delay-1 1.115, mixed-unseen 1.260,
mixed-composition 1.495, and high-damping 1.543.

The physical-support router is therefore frozen at context norm 1.2: use the
full intervention path at or above 1.2 and the no-intervention fallback below
1.2. The rule reads only the observable Z65 K25 posterior, never a residual or
domain label. Seed27 is development evidence for this new rule. Seeds37/47 are
the confirmation set and may not change the threshold or fallback.
