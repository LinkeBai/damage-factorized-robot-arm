# BT-DPWM paired safety and internal topology: Z15--Z17

## Z15: paired zero-reaction dominance

Every training trajectory and rollout depth is compared to the same model with
reaction disabled. Weight 2 gives seed-7 free +3.53%, object +25.58%, overall
+4.88%, with 1/4 regressions. Locked three-seed replication gives free +0.31%,
object +21.96%, overall +1.24%, with 7/12 regressions. Training dominance does
not transfer to held-out cells.

## Protocol audit

The V0--Z15 shared utility passed zero damage masks into every internal model
step and projected damage only outside the model. Thus BT-DPWM's internal action
masking, projection and topology messages had not actually been trained or
evaluated. New utilities support explicit internal topology conditioning while
keeping legacy experiments unchanged.

## Z16: full topology adaptation

Forty topology-aware robot epochs reduce seed-7 training loss by roughly 42%,
but primary free-arm performance is -101.47% relative to the strong baseline.
Updating the full scaffold on D2/D4 destroys leave-D3-out generalization.

## Z17: topology-input adapter

Only the mask and lock-angle input columns are trained: 272 existing weights,
zero initialized, with all other robot weights frozen. Seed 7 reaches primary
overall +6.50% and four-domain overall +3.58% with one regression. The full
three-seed audit gives free -2.40%, object +22.01%, overall -1.36%, and 7/12
regressions.

All candidates are NO-GO. Internal topology was a real protocol omission, but
its activation does not by itself establish repeatable improvement over the
h136/240 shared baseline.
