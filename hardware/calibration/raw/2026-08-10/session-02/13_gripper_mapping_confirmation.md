# Gripper Mapping Confirmation

- Servo ID: 6
- Physical function: gripper opening/closing
- Positive encoder direction: fingers move outward/open
- Return direction: fingers move inward/close
- J6 gripper-orientation joint: not present in the tested six-servo chain
- Mechanical zero and safe opening limits: not measured

This confirms the tested real arm has five positioning joints plus one gripper
actuator, not six positioning joints plus a separate gripper actuator.
