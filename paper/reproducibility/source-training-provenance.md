# Seed-27 source-model training provenance

Audit date: 2026-09-13. This is a bounded audit of existing configurations, summaries, checkpoint tensors, cache metadata and loader code. No fitting, simulation, model rollout, evaluation rerun or manuscript edit was performed. Paths below are relative to this document unless absolute.

## Finding and accounting boundary

The adaptation study starts from an already trained **robot and object predictor**, with the source geometric residual removed. The source recipe contains **40 ordinary training trajectories and 15 ordinary validation trajectories**, plus **320 decision-training groups and 80 decision-validation groups, with 32 candidates per group**. These are different accounting units: the latter imply **10,240 training and 2,560 validation candidate-outcome rows**, not 12,800 independent initial problems. A complete, deduplicated lifetime collection/development total is **not established** by the retained source summaries. The adaptation budgets must therefore exclude **all shared source-model training and development data**, rather than only nominal pretraining.

The [prior tensor audit](../../work/gap_closure/data-budget-evidence-audit.md:29) establishes that the strict-evaluation checkpoint used by adaptation is identical to the [primary seed-27 checkpoint](../../runs/icra_primary_decision_full_w10/seed27/model.pt). The adaptation [builder](../../scripts/ipwm_scale_train.py:25) always calls `final_models(27, ...)`; the [loader](../../scripts/audit_ipwm_targeted_advantage.py:29) loads the selected primary checkpoint, copies it and zeros its geometric residual. It does not load the later 50,000-pool adapted checkpoints.

## Initialization chain

1. **Shared baseline, z32.** The [z32 configuration](../../config/experiment/g2_bt_dpwm_meta_train_z32_v1.yaml:15) trains `baseline_model.pt` for 240 epochs at rollout horizon 5. The retained [seed-27 summary](../../runs/g2_bt_dpwm_meta_train_z32/seed27_v1/summary.json:10) contains exactly 240 baseline-history entries. This run also trains a separate scaffold and candidate; their optimization should not be attributed automatically to the exported baseline. The baseline is fitted on the topology/physics training split below, so “nominal” is a model label, not evidence of intact-only collection.
2. **z69 transfer.** The [z69 configuration](../../config/experiment/g2_bt_dpwm_zero_topology_columns_z69_v1.yaml:9) loads that baseline, copies robot weights, zeros topology input columns, uses zero robot epochs and 120 object epochs. Direct checkpoint comparison found all 20 compatible mapped robot tensors (318,378 values) equal to the baseline after the documented column zeroing. Its separate object training does not imply another trained robot initialization. The [z69 summary](../../runs/g2_bt_dpwm_zero_topology_columns_z69/seed27_v1/summary.json:15) selects object epoch 120.
3. **Strict pusher robot.** The [seed-27 configuration](../../config/experiment/g2_r0_strict_pusher_robot_seed27_development_v1.yaml:11) loads the z69 full template, drops object-to-robot input columns, and performs 40 robot-only epochs, with validation every two epochs and zero object epochs. Its [summary](../../runs/g2_r0_strict_pusher_robot/seed27_v1/summary.json:21) selects robot epoch 40. The loader implements the input-column reduction [here](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:921).
4. **Primary source.** The [primary configuration](../../config/experiment/icra_primary_d2d4_decision_development_3seed_v1.yaml:8) loads compatible robot tensors from the strict-pusher checkpoint, separately initializes matching `object_head.*` tensors from the z32 baseline, zeros robot topology-input columns again, and fits the object/residual part for 40 epochs with zero robot epochs. These are separate initialization paths, implemented in the [robot loader](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:939) and [baseline object-head copy](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:993). Direct comparison found all 20 primary robot tensors (317,834 values) equal to the strict-pusher source **after** zeroing `robot_encoder.0.weight[:, 3:5]`; before that adjustment, this one tensor differs. The [source summary](../../runs/icra_primary_decision_full_w10/seed27/summary.json:24) selects object epoch 40. Its `shared_epochs=10` field is not an additional ten training epochs: block-coordinate control flow uses the configured robot/object stages.

## Data units and evidence strength

| Component | Count supported by the retained evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary training recipe | 20 topology/physics domains × 2 trajectories = **40**, each 150 transitions | **6,000 recorded transitions**; reused by the configured stages, not a new collection per epoch |
| Ordinary validation recipe | 5 domains × 3 trajectories = **15**, each 150 transitions | **2,250 recorded transitions**; used for source-stage selection |
| Primary decision fitting | **320 groups**, all reported seen; 32 candidates/group | **10,240 candidate-outcome rows**, sharing initial states within groups |
| Primary decision validation | **80 groups**, 32 candidates/group | **2,560 candidate-outcome rows**, reused across epoch-selection checks |

The [q0a configuration](../../config/experiment/g2_bt_dpwm_meta_train_q0a_z32_v1.yaml:3) supplies 150 steps, two training trajectories per domain and three validation/test trajectories per domain. The [split](../../config/splits/g2_topology_meta_train_d1_d5_v1.yaml:7) contains 20 training domains across intact/D1/D2/D4/D5, and five validation domains: D1/D2/D4/D5 with weak motor and intact with noisy deadband. All four initialization-stage configurations reference this q0a recipe. The [collector](../../scripts/run_push_benchmark.py:171) creates the specified number per domain, cycling through target coordinates; target count is not an extra multiplicative factor. The primary driver uses training seed 270000 and validation seed 270750, before per-domain/per-trajectory offsets ([training](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:757), [validation](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:1226)).

Recomputing the current driver's documented cache keys identified retained [training cache](../../runs/trajectory_cache/42f0c3e06377f4e4a5df.pt) and [validation cache](../../runs/trajectory_cache/63fe09080d519562a629.pt). Read-only inspection confirms respectively 40 and 15 trajectories with the stated domain multiplicities, each with states `[151,14]` and actions `[150,5]`. All 55 concatenated state/action byte hashes are distinct. File SHA-256 values are respectively `ba9f123d94f67ee0911e934420ed06819c4376de67d2b3cbb447a736157d5ea1` and `375ad8468d45b1d609a15632d7f290c42d2770c1a33984d867b131e01672cbce`. These caches verify the retained recipe-sized data; historical source summaries do not record cache digests, so this is not proof that every earlier run used these exact bytes or that there were only 55 ordinary trajectories collected across development.

The source summary records [all 320 fitting groups seen](../../runs/icra_primary_decision_full_w10/seed27/summary.json:166) and [80 validation groups with 32 candidates](../../runs/icra_primary_decision_full_w10/seed27/summary.json:171). The [provenance ledger](../../reports/primary-result-provenance-ledger-20260831.md:10) identifies this as D2/D4 development. The [loader](../../src/robotarm/training/decision_focused.py:81) splits by `(locked_joint, episode)` before constructing groups; the primary configuration uses validation fraction 0.2 and split seed 7. Eight groups are consumed per epoch, and 40 epochs cover the reported 320 groups ([sampling](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:320)). Multiple groups may belong to one episode, so 400 groups must not be described as 400 independent episodes.

The exact **32-candidate training archive path/digest and its episode count are unresolved**: the configuration has no `decision_candidate_data` path, and the source summary does not retain the command-line archive argument. The retained [development metadata](../../data/primary_candidates/development_seed7.json:10) and [seed-27 V2 metadata](../../data/primary_candidates/development_v2_seed27.json:10) describe **128-candidate evaluation archives**, not the 32-candidate fitting archive. Their 400 groups, 80 episodes and 50-step horizon cannot be silently substituted as verified training metadata. The training loader repeats each segment action ten times, but the exact archived number of segments/unique source episodes remains unverified here.

Finally, one-step and multi-step losses reuse recorded trajectories. For example, primary ordinary object training applies H25 windows to each 150-step trajectory, yielding six windows per trajectory per epoch ([loss code](../../scripts/run_bt_dpwm_gate_y0.py:195)); these are **240 windows per epoch from 40 trajectories**, not 240 newly collected trials. The 40-epoch recipe therefore presents 9,600 such windows while reusing the same ordinary data. Repeated validation, upstream stage reuse, hyperparameter pilots and later evaluation must not be added as though they were new unique training trajectories. The [ledger](../../reports/primary-result-provenance-ledger-20260831.md:12) records exploratory pilots and prior domain inspection, but does not provide a complete collection census.

## Optional paper insertion (two sentences)

“All adaptation fits reuse the seed-27 primary model's trained robot and object dynamics; its recorded source recipe uses 40 ordinary training and 15 validation trajectories, plus 320 decision-training and 80 decision-validation groups with 32 candidates per group. The reported adaptation budgets exclude all shared source-model training and development data; candidate groups and repeated trajectory windows are not independent trials, and a complete deduplicated lifetime collection total is unavailable.”
